About me

I am a senior Artificial Intelligence student at Effat University with a strong foundation in machine learning, deep learning, natural language processing and data analytics. I am passionate about turning data into intelligent, practical solutions for real-world problems.

My project work centres on healthcare AI, including early preeclampsia prediction, wearable-based seizure detection, and Badira, a preeclampsia awareness app developed for the StartSmart Saudi University Challenge. I hold the IBM AI Engineering Professional Certificate and am committed to continuously learning and applying emerging technologies in this fast-evolving field.
