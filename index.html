    About me

    I'm a senior Artificial Intelligence student at Effat University in Jeddah. I like turning data into tools people can actually use, and I care as much about whether a model can be trusted as about how well it scores.

    Most of my work is in healthcare: predicting preeclampsia from maternal health data, detecting seizures with wearable sensors, and Badira, a bilingual app that helps Arab women recognise preeclampsia risk early. I recently completed IBM's AI Engineering certificate, and this semester I'm taking Deep Learning and Natural Language Processing.

    Outside class I volunteer at Ramadan food drives and community events, and this summer I helped run a children's sports camp.
